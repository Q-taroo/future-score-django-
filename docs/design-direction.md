# FUTURE SCORE — Future Commons

Concept: 未来を考える楽しさ。A welcoming place to form a view, compare and learn;
not a trading terminal or a prize-based game.

## Visual system

- Blue #2563EB: primary action and identity. Navy #14213D: reading.
- Cyan: light, exploratory backgrounds. Amber: discovery and milestones, not urgency.
- Green/pink retain YES/NO meanings; always pair with labels, never color alone.
- System sans-serif with Japanese fallbacks; no third-party font download or extra tracking.
- Rounded cards, restrained depth, stable layouts; no flashing or compulsory motion.
- Break at 900px for hero and navigation; wrap controls and preserve readable text.
- Illustration is HTML/CSS with real text, avoiding cropped raster lettering and easing translation.
- Fictional preview is explicitly descriptive, never fabricated live statistics.
